Fabric Low-Level Design

Fabric low-level design

Warsaw 3 and Warsaw 2 active-active, Madrid DR. EVPN-VXLAN spine-leaf with an eBGP underlay and overlay, 25 country VRFs on every leaf, inter-VRF only through the INT/BAS firewall.

Design decisions to confirm

    Inter-VRF and inter-site traffic

    country3 to country7, same site
    1. Leaf has no route to country7 in VRF country3, so it follows the VRF default route (EVPN type-5 from the local gateways).
    2. Gateway sends it to the INT/BAS firewall on the country3 transit VLAN.
    3. Firewall applies the zone policy and routes it into the country7 transit VLAN.
    4. Gateway in VRF country7 sends it over VXLAN (L3VNI 50007) to the destination leaf.
    country3, Warsaw 3 to Warsaw 2
    1. Same VRF, so no firewall. The leaf learned the Warsaw 2 prefix as a type-5 route re-originated by its local gateways.
    2. Traffic goes VXLAN to the local gateway pair, which re-encapsulates towards the Warsaw 2 gateways over the DCI underlay (EVPN multi-domain gateway).
    3. Each site keeps its own default route: 0.0.0.0/0 is filtered on the gateway-to-gateway EVPN sessions.
    Country VRF to WAN or internet
    1. Default route points to the INT/BAS firewall.
    2. INT/BAS firewall reaches the carrier WAN through the DCI VRF WAN, and the internet through the DMZ firewall on the transit VLAN.
    3. The DCI default VRF carries only the fabric underlay between sites. Country prefixes never enter it.
