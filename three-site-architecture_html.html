Three-site datacenter architecture

Warsaw 3 and Warsaw 2 run active-active, and Madrid is the DR site. This combines the high-level drawing (inter-site links, storage, AS400) with the three site detail drawings. Amber dashed outlines and numbered badges mark items I couldn't confirm from the drawings; the notes below explain each one.

Legend

  • Dark fibre (IP), Warsaw 3 to Warsaw 2
  • Dark fibre (FC), PowerMax replication
  • Warsaw to Madrid DCI link
  • SD-WAN internet underlay
  • SD-WAN to DCI
  • OOB uplink (Starlink / 5G)
  • Uncertain link
  • L2: MLAG peer-link, 2 links per pair
  • L2: firewall HA / state sync
  • L3 intra-site link (gray mesh = routed spine-leaf underlay)

Uncertain or unconfirmed items

  1. 1Internet edge: one ISP or two, and is it BGP on your own PI space? Not shown on the detail drawings.
  2. 2Both Warsaw 3 SD-WAN routers are labelled 199.204.10.100/32. Is that a duplicate IP or a drawing copy-paste?
  3. 3Where the DCI lands in the fabric is unclear. Warsaw 2 seems to go via the INT/BAS firewalls, Warsaw 3 seems to go straight to GW102, and Madrid seems to go to both the INT/BAS firewalls and blf102.
  4. 4In Warsaw 3, the last two leaves are labelled L109/L110 a second time. They're shown here as L111/L112.
  5. 5Storage, compute and AS400 come from the high-level drawing only. Which leaves they attach to isn't shown.
  6. 6OOB now uses a Zscaler device (replacing the OOB firewall), uplinked over Starlink and 5G so management access does not depend on the site's own internet or WAN. The appliance is a Zscaler Zero Trust Branch (ZTB) appliance, with the model still to size. It is one appliance per site.
  7. 7SD-WAN routers are labelled 'SDWAN A&T', probably AT&T. Is the underlay diverse?
  8. 8Warsaw 2's fourth spine is labelled MARfab1-S104, probably copied from the Madrid template.
  9. 9Madrid shows a Zain cloud but no Zain CE router.
  10. 10Madrid naming is mixed: mad1fab1 for the border leaves, MARfab1 for spines and leaves.
  11. 11Warsaw to Madrid: the high-level drawing shows one L3 10 Gbps link per Warsaw DCI, while the Madrid detail shows four numbered 'DCI links dark fibre'. The diagram assumes each Warsaw DCI switch lands on a different Madrid core, so each site survives the loss of one Madrid core; the Madrid drawing only shows links 1-2 on core-01 and 3-4 on core-02.
